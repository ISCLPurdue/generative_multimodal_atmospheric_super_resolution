# 2019 interface-development records

The selected interface is recorded in
`reproduction/config/selected_interface_2019.json`.  The files under
`development_history/` preserve the frozen candidate inventories, selection
rules, grid-boundary checks, and checksums used during 2019 development.

Names such as `legacy_keep015`, Stage III/IV, `superob`, and `strat24` are
historical experiment identifiers.  They are retained to keep the selection
lineage auditable; they are not names for the final R/A/S interfaces and
are not the entry points for the 2020 paper comparisons.
